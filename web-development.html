How long does a SaaS build take?
A focused MVP typically ships in 8–12 weeks. Larger platforms run 12–20 weeks. You get a detailed timeline after discovery.
Can you take over an existing codebase?
Yes. We start with a code audit, document what we find and agree a plan to stabilise or refactor before adding features.
