From 2,000 daily tickets to an AI that handles it all

The Problem: Horizon’s support team was buried under 2,000+ tickets per day. Response times hit 48 hours, churn spiked to 12%, and hiring more agents wasn’t sustainable.
Our Solution: We built a RAG-powered AI assistant trained on their entire knowledge base, API docs, and historical support threads — integrated directly into their support widget.

Ask Us Anything
How long does a typical project take?
It depends on scope. A marketing website takes 3–6 weeks. A full-featured SaaS platform typically runs 12–20 weeks. We always provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase so you know exactly what to expect before we start building.
What does your development process look like?
We work in two-week agile sprints. Each sprint ends with a live demo so you can see real progress, give feedback, and steer the direction. You’re never left wondering what we’re working on — we use shared project boards and weekly video calls throughout the build.
Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?
Yes. All projects come with a 30-day free bug-fix warranty after launch. Beyond that, we offer flexible monthly retainers for ongoing support, feature development, performance monitoring, and security updates. Many clients keep us on retainer long-term as their dedicated engineering team.
How do you price your projects?
We offer fixed-price project quotes for well-defined scopes, and time-and-materials contracts for exploratory or evolving work. There are no hidden fees. After an initial discovery call, we provide a transparent breakdown before you commit to anything.
Will we own all the code and IP after the project?
Absolutely. Upon final payment, you receive full ownership of all source code, assets, and intellectual property. We hand over everything — repositories, design files, deployment configurations, and documentation. There are no licensing restrictions or lock-ins.
Can you work with our existing codebase or team?
Yes. We regularly integrate with existing engineering teams as an extension, take over legacy codebases, or pick up mid-project work. We begin every engagement with a thorough code audit so we understand exactly what we’re building on before we touch anything.
